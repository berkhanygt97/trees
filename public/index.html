    HOW TO PLAY

    🌱 FARMING

    Walk onto your field and look at a tile. E does the next job: plow, plant, water, harvest. Hold it and walk along a row.

    1–7 or the mouse wheel picks which seed to plant. Watered crops grow 50% faster. Rain waters everything for free.

    💰 MONEY

    Sell at the Market in town for full price, or drop it in the shipping bin on your farm for 80%.

    Prices drift every morning, and flooding the market with one crop drags its price down. The Orders board pays a bonus.

    🚜 MACHINES

    Buy a tractor at the Tractor Barn, then a plow, seeder or water tank. Drive over your field and it works three rows at once. G swaps implement.

    The combine harvests five rows. F gets in and out, V changes camera.

    🏡 GROWING UP

    Builders upgrade your house (more storage) and build a mill, dairy and bakery. The Land Office sells bigger fields. Cluck & Moo sells coops, barns and animals.

    📐 PLAN YOUR FARM

    At your house, Plan my farm: drag buildings and the field around a blueprint of your plot; R turns a building. $100 per thing moved.

    🧑‍🌾 HIRED HANDS

    The Job Centre (south end of main street) has new faces every morning. Pick a job, give them a name, and they work whenever the host is running. Wages come out each morning.

    🍔 RESTAURANTS & TILLS

    From farm level 4 the Land Office sells the lots on your own hood street: open a burger joint, pizzeria or bakery café (more of them as you level up). Stock the pantry from your farm, cook and serve at the counter or hire staff. From restaurant level 3, take phone orders and race them out on your scooter.

    Takings go into the till, banked every four in-game hours (or BANK IT at the counter). A full till is what raiders come for.

    🏚️ YOUR HOOD

    Your street has your farm, your restaurant lots, six houses, a park and your clubhouse. At the clubhouse door: rename your gang, buy upgrades (a bigger clubhouse, an armoury, walls, lookouts, safes, palm trees, a billboard, done-up houses), and pay for repairs.

    Everyone you hire joins your gang. Soldiers (Job Centre) patrol your street with guns while you are in the valley.

    🚨 RIVAL GANGS

    Los Coyotes, the Dust Devils MC and the Neon Serpents raid hoods worth robbing — only while their boss is online. They crack tills, tag walls, smash buildings and trample crops. Drop a raider with a bag and it falls where they stood: pick it up to put the money back. If their car gets away, so does the money.

    Rival tags cost you customers: hold E at the wall to scrub them off.

    ⚔️ GANG WARS

    Declare war on another boss from your clubhouse. After a minute's warning you have six minutes in their hood: hold E to tag walls (+3, and more while they stay up), crack tills and get the bag home (+1 per $500), smash buildings (+2). Wasting the other boss is +2, downing their gang +1. Ties go to the defender. The winner takes the pot and 20% of the loser's takings for a day.

    🦺 GETTING HURT

    Rusty's sells a vest (the white bar) and city guns: a 9mm and a machine pistol (hold to fire). At zero health you are wasted and come round at your clubhouse a few seconds later, dropping any bag you carried.

    🎰 THE CASINO

    North of the plaza. Same money as everything else. A rotating event is always about to start.

    🏁 DRIVING

    Cars have real suspension and grip: Space is the handbrake for drifting, ramps send you flying, walls hurt. Bins and hydrants go flying too. The speedway is past the casino, north. V swaps the chase camera for the driver's seat.

    🐗 WILD BOARS

    Every so often a herd charges out of the woods and starts eating your crops. Q gets Grandpa's rifle out, RMB aims, LMB fires, R reloads. Headshots count double.

    Get close and a boar paws the ground and grunts — that is your warning. Then it charges in a straight line: step sideways and it runs past. Only one comes at you at a time. Get hit too often and you are wasted (you lose nothing).

    Boars get bigger and meaner as your farm levels up; Rusty's Guns in town sells better guns. Or just run them over.

    🗺️ THE MAP

    M opens the whole valley: every hood and whose gang runs it, other players, raiders, your clubhouse and restaurants. Wheel to zoom, drag to move.

    Click to plant a waypoint: it shows on your radar (on the rim when it is far away) until you get there. Click it again to take it away. N turns the sound on and off.

    🖥️ GRAPHICS

    P cycles Auto, Ultra, High, Medium and Low. Auto picks for your machine and steps down if the game slows. F3 shows the frame rate and the setting.

    Everything saves on the host's computer automatically. Come back with the same name to pick up where you left off.

    GAME Q or ESC to leave
